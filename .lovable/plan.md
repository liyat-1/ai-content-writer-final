# Content release workspace refinement

## Goal
Make Content feel like one polished calendar workspace, remove unnecessary AI popups, and make generated releases publishable without mandatory review.

## Changes
- Combine the month controls, version details, personalization banner, and campaign groups into one continuous calendar surface.
- Rename the page action to **Edit content with AI** and open the AI workspace directly.
- Show the AI workspace within the page while keeping the current campaign context visible, rather than as a blocking popup.
- Replace the default-content choice popup with an inline gradient personalization banner above the campaign cards.
- Expand the AI plan into a real campaign strategy covering timeframe, audiences, channels, events, holidays, imagery, templates, and performance learnings.
- Repair attachment selection so adding documents, spreadsheets, images, or video does not freeze the screen.
- Restore a richer animated generation experience instead of a single plain status card.
- Treat generated work as a timeframe-based release: hide live calendar controls until publishing, show **Publish release** immediately, and keep review optional.
- Give generated cards one **Review** action only, remove Test, and replace misleading author/date text with an **AI generated** label.
- Put the campaign AI editor directly inside the review panel; remove the second “Open AI editor” popup.

## Verification
- Check default/live calendar states, direct AI entry, attachment selection, plan approval, generation, optional review, and direct publishing.
- Confirm desktop and narrow layouts, no freezes, and no console or build errors.
