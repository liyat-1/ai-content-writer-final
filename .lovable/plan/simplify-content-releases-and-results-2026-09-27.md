# Simplify Content, Releases, and Results

## Goal
Make the Content area easy to understand around one idea: hotels start with Directful’s default content, publish seasonal updates for a defined timeframe, and compare that publication with the matching prior period.

## Published content
- Return to a focused single-timeframe view instead of a multi-month dashboard.
- Keep the selected month and its live/default status above the campaign cards.
- Show the campaign cards directly, with their channel and guest previews, Test, Edit content, and version history.
- Keep the seasonal AI prompt in context above the campaigns, without adding new publishing or version concepts.

## Releases
- Use a compact publication selector grouped by year.
- Show only the selected publication’s timeframe, status, publication date, summary, and campaign coverage.
- Explain what changed and why, followed by the campaigns included in that publication.
- Remove unnecessary month-result cards, metrics, and layered navigation from this page.

## Results
- Require a publication/timeframe selection and make that selection prominent.
- Compare the selected publication only with its matching prior-year timeframe.
- Use the meeting metrics: click rate, click-to-book, and spam rate.
- Show a concise overall comparison, then monthly and per-campaign breakdowns for that same timeframe.
- Add AI explanations for increases and decreases while distinguishing likely content impact from seasonality, weather, and other external factors.
- Remove unrelated metrics, confidence labels, voting, version controls, and extra drill-down screens.

## Validation
- Check Content, Releases, and Results on desktop and mobile.
- Confirm publication switching updates every heading, comparison, metric, and insight consistently.
- Confirm the preview builds without errors.
